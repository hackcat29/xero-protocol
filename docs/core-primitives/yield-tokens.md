# Yield Tokens (YT)

A **Yield Token (YT)** entitles its holder to all cash dividend distributions and multiplier growth generated by the underlying tokenized stock from the moment of purchase until the maturity date $T$.

---

## 🏷️ Naming Convention

Xero Yield Tokens are issued as standard ERC-20 tokens following the format:

$$\text{x}[SYMBOL]\text{-YT-}[TENOR]$$

* Example: `xNVDAc-YT-3M` (Yield Token for NVIDIA Corp, maturing in 3 months)
* Example: `xSCHDc-YT-1Y` (Yield Token for Schwab US Dividend Equity ETF, maturing in 1 year)

---

## 💰 How YT Works

When an investor buys or holds a Yield Token:
1. **Dividend Growth Rights:** The holder receives 100% of the economic benefit of every dividend distribution declared by the company during the tenor.
2. **Streaming Continuous Claims:** Whenever the underlying B20 stock's multiplier increments, the vault updates its internal `dividendIndex`. YT holders can invoke `claimYield(address user)` at any time to withdraw accrued dividend shares directly to their wallet.
3. **No Liquidation Risk:** YT gives leveraged exposure to stock dividends without debt, margins, or liquidation thresholds. You cannot be liquidated.
4. **Natural Expiry:** Once the maturity timestamp $T$ passes, the dividend window closes. The YT reaches maturity, ceases to accrue yield, and has an intrinsic terminal value of $0.

---

## ⚡ The Power of Cash Flow Leverage

Because dividend yields on blue-chip equities typically range between 0.5% and 4.0% annually, a Yield Token trades at only a tiny fraction of the underlying spot stock price:

$$\text{Leverage Multiplier} = \frac{P_{spot}}{P_{YT}}$$

| Asset | Spot Price ($P_{spot}$) | YT Price ($P_{YT}$, 1Y) | Capital Leverage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`NVDAc`** | $130.00 | $1.20 | **$108.3\times$** |
| **`AAPLc`** | $230.00 | $2.10 | **$109.5\times$** |
| **`SPYc`** | $580.00 | $7.25 | **$80.0\times$** |
| **`SCHDc`** | $82.00 | $2.85 | **$28.8\times$** |

### Worked Example: Dividend Hike Speculation
Suppose `NVDAc` trades at **$130.00**, and the market expects a regular dividend of **$0.30** per quarter ($1.20/year).
* An investor purchases **1,000 `xNVDAc-YT-1Y`** for **$1,200** (cost: $1.20 per YT).
* To obtain the same dividend exposure by buying spot stock, the investor would need to deploy **$130,000** (1,000 shares @ $130.00).
* **Corporate Action:** NVIDIA announces a blockbuster quarter and raises its quarterly dividend to **$0.60** ($2.40/year total).
* **Outcome:**
  * Spot stock investor earns $2,400 dividend on $130,000 capital $\implies$ **+1.85% return**.
  * YT investor earns $2,400 dividend on $1,200 capital $\implies$ **+100.0% net profit ($2\times$ return)**!

{% hint style="success" %}
**Capital Efficiency:** Yield Tokens allow traders and dividend hedge funds to express high-conviction views on corporate dividend announcements using 1% to 3% of the capital normally required in TradFi.
{% endhint %}

## 📉 Time Decay ($\theta$) Dynamics

Yield Tokens are decaying assets. The fair market value of a YT obeys the Yield-Space relationship:

$$P_{YT} = r \times \tau$$

Where:
* $r$ is the market-implied dividend rate (annualized).
* $\tau = \frac{T - t}{365 \text{ days}}$ is the time-to-maturity in fractional years.

As time elapses ($t \to T$), $\tau$ monotonically declines to $0$. Even if the market's expectation of the company's annual dividend rate remains constant, the market price of the YT smoothly drifts downward towards zero as dividend payment dates pass.

---

## 🛡️ Risk Profile

* **Dividend Cut Risk:** If a corporation reduces or suspends its dividend payout, the total cash flows distributed to the YT holder will decrease, potentially resulting in a loss on the purchased YT.
* **Expiration (Theta) Decay:** Unlike holding spot stock, holding YT to maturity results in complete token depreciation. If accrued dividends do not exceed the purchase price of the YT, the position will be net negative.
* **Smart Contract Risk:** Accrued dividends are held within [`XeroVault.sol`](../developers/smart-contract-reference.md#xerovaultsol) until claimed. The contract is non-custodial and verified against reentrancy.
