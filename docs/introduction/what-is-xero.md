# What is Xero Protocol?

**Xero Protocol** is an autonomous, non-custodial decentralized protocol designed to bring fixed-income and yield-derivatives primitives to tokenized equities on **Base**.

In traditional finance, equity derivatives—such as dividend strips, dividend futures, and stripped bonds—are restricted to prime brokers, hedge funds, and institutional desks. Xero democratizes this entire financial layer by enabling permissionless stripping, trading, and speculation on the dividend cash flows of real-world tokenized stocks.

---

## 🎯 The Core Problem with Tokenized Equities

Tokenized stocks (such as Coinbase B20 assets on Base) allow global investors to hold 1:1 backed representations of major public corporations (e.g., Apple, NVIDIA, Coinbase, Tesla). 

However, prior to Xero, tokenized equity holders faced a major limitation:
* **All-or-Nothing Exposure:** An investor holding a tokenized stock is exposed simultaneously to both **capital price volatility** and **dividend cash flows**.
* **No Way to Lock In Yield:** If a stock yields 4% annually in dividends, there was no on-chain mechanism to sell those future dividends upfront to lock in a guaranteed fixed return.
* **No Way to Speculate on Dividends Without Price Risk:** If an investor anticipates an upcoming dividend hike or special cash distribution, they had to purchase the full expensive underlying share, exposing themselves to downside stock price movement.

---

## 💡 The Xero Solution

Xero solves this by establishing a mathematically verifiable yield-stripping engine:

```
  1 B20 Stock Token (Spot)  ───►  [XERO VAULT]  ───►  1 Principal Token (PT)
                                                └───►  1 Yield Token (YT)
```

1. **You Keep the Stock:** Holding **PT** ensures you can redeem your underlying share 1:1 upon maturity.
2. **You Trade the Dividends:** Holding **YT** captures all ongoing dividend compounding generated by the stock.
3. **Guaranteed Recombination:** At any point prior to maturity, holding 1 PT + 1 YT allows you to recombine and withdraw your original B20 stock token instantly.

{% hint style="success" %}
**Solvency by Construction:**  
Because 1 PT + 1 YT is minted by locking exactly 1 unit of underlying collateral, Xero vaults are mathematically solvent at every block.
{% endhint %}

## 🌐 Why Base?

Xero is built natively on **Base** to take advantage of:
* **Coinbase B20 Standard:** Direct compatibility with the official B20 tokenized stock infrastructure and Chainlink Total Return Equity feeds.
* **Low Latency & Sub-Cent Fees:** Yield claims and micro-arbitrage on AMM curves require low gas overhead, making Base's OP Stack L2 the ideal home for high-frequency RWA derivatives.
* **Deep Ecosystem Composability:** Seamless integration with Base liquidity hubs (Aerodrome), lending markets (Aave on Base), and self-custodial wallets (Coinbase Wallet).
