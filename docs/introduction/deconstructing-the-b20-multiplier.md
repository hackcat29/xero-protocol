# Deconstructing the B20 Multiplier

The foundation of Xero Protocol is the mathematical deconstruction of the **B20 Multiplier ($M_t$)**.

---

## 🔬 Understanding the Multiplier ($M_t$)

In the Coinbase B20 standard, a tokenized stock has two balances:
1. **Raw Balance (`balanceOf`):** The internal storage balance, which stays constant unless tokens are minted, transferred, or burned.
2. **Scaled Balance (`balanceOfUI`):** The real-world share entitlement, computed dynamically using the token's `uiMultiplier`:

$$\text{Share Entitlement} = \frac{\text{balanceOf} \times M_t}{10^{18}}$$

When a public company declares a cash dividend, the custody broker automatically reinvests the dividend cash into shares at the prevailing market price ($P_{share}$). This increases the token's multiplier on-chain:

$$\Delta M = \frac{\text{Dividend per Share}}{P_{share}} \times M_{current}$$

---

## ✂️ The Deconstruction Mechanism

When an investor deposits a B20 tokenized stock into [`XeroVault`](../developers/smart-contract-reference.md#xerovaultsol), the vault locks the underlying token and issues two separate, time-bound ERC-20 tokens maturing at timestamp $T$:

```
                      [Deposit 1 B20 Stock Token at Multiplier M_0]
                                            │
                                            ▼
               ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
               │                      Xero Vault                        │
               └────────────────────┬──────────────┬────────────────────┘
                                    │              │
                   Mints 1 PT       ▼              ▼       Mints 1 YT
                    ┌──────────────────┐        ┌──────────────────┐
                    │ Principal Token  │        │   Yield Token    │
                    │       (PT)       │        │       (YT)       │
                    └────────┬─────────┘        └────────┬─────────┘
                             │                           │
                             ▼                           ▼
                     Entitles holder to          Entitles holder to
                     baseline capital at         all multiplier growth
                     maturity (T)                ΔM = M_current - M_0
```

### 1. Principal Token (PT)
* **Entitlement:** Entitles the holder to redeem the underlying stock's baseline capital value at maturity $T$, stripped of all dividend accruals during the vault tenor.
* **Pricing:** Because the dividend growth is stripped out, PT trades on the secondary market at a discount to the spot share price ($P_{PT} < P_{spot}$).

### 2. Yield Token (YT)
* **Entitlement:** Entitles the holder to stream and claim all dividend multiplier increases ($\Delta M$) generated by the stock between deposit and maturity $T$.
* **Pricing:** Because YT reflects only the expected dividend flow over the time horizon (typically 1.5% to 4% of share value), it is priced at a small fraction of the share price, offering **50x–120x cash flow leverage**.

## 🔄 The Token Lifecycle

{% tabs %}
{% tab title="1. Strip" %}
**Deposit & Mint**  
The investor deposits $X$ raw stock tokens at dividend index $I_0$.  
The vault locks the tokens and mints:
* $X$ Principal Tokens (`xTICKER-PT-Maturity`)
* $X$ Yield Tokens (`xTICKER-YT-Maturity`)
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="2. Pre-Maturity (Trading & Accrual)" %}
* **Hold PT:** The holder passively waits for maturity to capture the fixed discount.
* **Trade YT:** The holder can sell YT immediately on the AMM to lock in cash yield upfront.
* **Hold YT:** The holder earns ongoing dividends via the Streaming Delta Index and calls `claimYield()` at any time.
* **Recombine:** Any user holding equal units of PT and YT can burn them to withdraw the original stock token at any time before maturity.
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="3. Maturity & Redemption" %}
At maturity timestamp $T$:
* **YT Expires:** YT stops accruing further dividends and trading on the AMM halts.
* **PT Redemption Opens:** PT holders call `redeem()` to burn PT and receive the full underlying stock shares.
{% endtab %}
{% endtabs %}
