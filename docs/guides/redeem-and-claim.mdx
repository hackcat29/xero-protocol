title: "Redeem and claim"
description: "Get your shares back, rejoin tokens early, and collect dividends."
---

## Redeem share tokens at maturity

On or after the maturity date, open **Portfolio**. Matured positions show **Redeem**. Each share token redeems for the tokenized shares worth one share. There's no deadline, and dividends paid after maturity stay with you.

## Rejoin before maturity

If you hold both a share token and a dividend token for the same market, you can rejoin them into the tokenized stock at any time before maturity, in the **Redeem** tab of the market's ticket. You get back exactly the shares the pair represents.

## Claim dividends

Dividend tokens collect dividends as they're reinvested. Claim them from the **Redeem** tab of a market, or all at once with **Claim** on **Portfolio**. Dividends are paid in the tokenized stock. Claims never expire, and transferring dividend tokens settles what each side had earned up to that moment.

## If a market is paused

| Situation | What still works |
| --- | --- |
| Paused for an unreported stock split | Rejoining, claiming dividends already collected, withdrawing liquidity |
| New deposits halted after an unusual corporate action | Rejoining, redeeming, claiming, withdrawing liquidity |
| Emergency pause by the guardian | Rejoining, redeeming, claiming, withdrawing liquidity |

No pause can trap a position. At worst, redeeming at maturity waits until a pending stock split has been reported.
