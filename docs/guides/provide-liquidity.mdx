---
title: "Provide liquidity"
description: "Earn trading fees and dividends by funding a dividend market."
---

Every market's prices come from a pool holding dividend tokens and the tokenized stock. Liquidity providers fund that pool and earn:

- the **0.3% fee** on every trade, and
- the **dividends** collected by the dividend tokens in the pool, which are added to the pool automatically.

## How to add liquidity

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get dividend tokens">
    In a market's ticket, open **More → Split shares** to split tokenized stock into share tokens and dividend tokens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add to the pool">
    Open **More → Add liquidity**, enter the dividend tokens to add, and the ticket shows the tokenized stock needed to match the pool's current ratio. Confirm. You receive pool shares.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Withdraw any time">
    **More → Remove liquidity** returns your share of both sides. Withdrawals work in every state, including after maturity and during pauses.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Risks

If the expected dividend yield moves, the pool's mix of dividend tokens and stock changes, and you may end up with less value than if you'd held the tokens yourself. Dividend tokens in the pool also lose value as maturity approaches, offset by the dividends they collect. Keep the share tokens from step 1: they redeem for shares at maturity.
