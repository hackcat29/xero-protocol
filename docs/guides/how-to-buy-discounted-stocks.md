# How to Buy Discounted Stocks

Buying **Principal Tokens (PT)** allows you to acquire blue-chip US equities at a guaranteed discount below their current market price, locking in an annualized fixed yield denominated in equity shares.

---

## 🖥️ Step-by-Step Walkthrough

### Step 1: Visit the Discounted Equities Dashboard
Navigate to [xeroprotocol.com](https://xeroprotocol.com) and click on **Discounted Stocks**.

### Step 2: Select Your Desired Equity & Tenor
Browse through the available assets:

| Asset | Tenor | Spot Price | PT Price | Discount | Fixed APY |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`xNVDAc-PT-6M`** | 182 Days | $130.00 | $124.50 | **4.2%** | **8.62%** |
| **`xCOINc-PT-6M`** | 182 Days | $220.00 | $211.20 | **4.0%** | **8.20%** |
| **`xSPYc-PT-1Y`** | 365 Days | $580.00 | $552.00 | **4.8%** | **5.07%** |

### Step 3: Select Payment Currency
You can purchase PT using:
* **USDC** (routed via Base DEX aggregators)
* **Underlying B20 Stock Tokens** (direct AMM swap)

### Step 4: Confirm Swap & Receive PT
Enter the number of PT tokens to purchase. Review the slippage tolerance, implied fixed APY, and maturity date, then click **Buy PT**.

---

## 🏆 Holding to Maturity & Redemption

1. **Self-Custody:** Keep the PT tokens in your self-custody wallet (or use them in supported Base lending protocols).
2. **At Maturity Timestamp ($T$):** 
   * Navigate to the **Portfolio & Redemptions** page.
   * Click **Redeem Stock**.
   * Your PT tokens are burned, and **1 full underlying stock share per PT** is credited to your wallet.

{% hint style="success" %}
**Zero Dividend Uncertainty:** Regardless of whether the company cut, raised, or canceled its dividend during the tenor, your PT entitles you to 100% of the underlying stock share upon maturity.
{% endhint %}
