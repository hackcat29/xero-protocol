---
title: "Trade dividends"
description: "Buy or sell a stock's dividend stream on its own."
---

Each market lets you trade the dividend token directly. Buying it gets you every dividend one share pays until maturity, for a fraction of the share price.

## When it makes sense

- You expect a company to **raise its dividend**, or pay a special dividend, more than the market expects.
- You want income from a stock without paying for the whole share.

You profit if the dividends actually paid beat the **expected dividend yield** shown in the market. If they fall short, you lose some or all of what you paid.

## Example

The Microsoft 1-year dividend token costs about $3.77 and the market expects a 0.71% yield. If Microsoft pays 0.80% over the year instead, the dividends you collect are worth about $4.25, or $4.12 after Xero's 3% fee: a 9% gain. If it pays 0.60%, you collect about $3.09 after the fee and lose about 18%.

<Warning>
  Dividend tokens are worth nothing once their dividends have been collected, and their price decays as maturity approaches. Small changes in dividends cause large changes in their value.
</Warning>

## How to trade

Open a stock from **Markets**, choose a maturity, and use the **Dividends** tab in the ticket. Choose **Buy dividends** or **Sell dividends**, enter an amount, and confirm. The ticket shows the expected yield before and after your trade, the price impact and the minimum you'll receive.

Collected dividends appear under **Redeem → Dividends earned**, and can be claimed at any time.
