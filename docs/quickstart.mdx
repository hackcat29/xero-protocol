title: "Quickstart"
description: "Claim free test tokens and buy your first stock at a discount on Base Sepolia."
---

Xero runs on Base Sepolia, a free test network. This walkthrough takes about five minutes and costs nothing.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get a wallet">
    Any Ethereum wallet works: Coinbase Wallet, MetaMask, Rainbow, or anything that supports WalletConnect. Open [xeroprotocol.com/app](https://xeroprotocol.com/app) and select **Connect**. Home shows a short checklist that tracks the next steps for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get test ETH for gas">
    Transactions on Base cost a fraction of a cent, but you still need a little test ETH. Claim some from the [Coinbase Base Sepolia faucet](https://www.coinbase.com/faucets/base-ethereum-sepolia-faucet).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim test stock">
    Select **Get test tokens** in the sidebar, then claim any of the six stocks and ETFs. Each claim gives you about $100 worth, once a day per stock.

    <Tip>On a phone, use the gift button in the top bar, or the link that appears in the order ticket when your wallet is empty.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy at a discount">
    Go to **Buy at a discount**, pick a stock (SCHD has the largest dividend, so the largest discount), and pick a date. Enter an amount, select **Review order**, check the summary and select **Confirm purchase**. The first time, your wallet also asks you to approve the token.

    You now hold a share token that becomes your shares on the date you chose. See it under **Portfolio**, and the transaction under **Activity**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or take dividends upfront">
    Go to **Dividends upfront**, pick a stock you hold and a date, then review and confirm. The payout lands in your wallet straight away, and your shares come back to you on that date.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you'll see in your wallet

| Token | What it is | Example name |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tokenized stock | One share of the stock, with past dividends reinvested | `SCHDc` |
| Share token | Becomes the share at maturity | `xSCHDc-PT-1Y` |
| Dividend token | Collects the dividends until maturity | `xSCHDc-YT-1Y` |

In the app these appear as plain names, like "SCHD share token · Oct 2027".

<Note>
  `SCHDc` and the other test stocks are mock tokens that behave like tokenized stocks on Base. They are not real shares and have no value.
</Note>

<Tip>Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>) anywhere in the app to search for a stock or page.</Tip>
