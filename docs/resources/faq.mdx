---
title: "FAQ"
description: "Common questions about Xero."
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is Xero a broker?">
    No. Xero is open-source software running on the Base network. You trade directly from your own wallet, and Xero never holds your funds outside the public vault contracts.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need an account?">
    No sign-up, no KYC on Xero itself. You need a wallet and tokenized stocks.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where does the discount come from?">
    From the dividends. Buying at a discount means buying the share without its dividends until your chosen date. Someone else sold those dividends upfront, so you pay less. See [How prices are set](/concepts/pricing).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is the discount guaranteed?">
    The price you pay is fixed when you buy, and on the maturity date you receive the shares. Your dollar return still depends on the share price, as with any stock.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is the payout for dividends upfront in the stock, not dollars?">
    The dividend market trades dividends against the tokenized stock itself, so that's what you're paid in. You can keep it or swap it for dollars or stablecoins elsewhere.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I lose access to my wallet?">
    Your tokens are in your wallet, so the same rules apply as for any crypto asset. Xero can't recover them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="When is Xero coming to mainnet?">
    Xero is live on Base Sepolia today, and its mainnet deployment is prepared. Follow [GitHub](https://github.com/hackcat29/xero-protocol) for the launch.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who built Xero?">
    Xero is open source. The code, tests and deployment scripts are on [GitHub](https://github.com/hackcat29/xero-protocol).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
