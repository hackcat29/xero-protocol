---
title: "Glossary"
description: "Xero's terms, in plain language and in contract terms."
---

| Term | Plain meaning | In the contracts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Tokenized stock** | A token tracking a company's shares, with dividends reinvested | The underlying, an ERC-8056 token with `uiMultiplier()` |
| **Multiplier** | How many shares one token represents | `uiMultiplier()`, `M` |
| **Share token** | Becomes the share at maturity | Principal token, `PT`, e.g. `xMSFTc-PT-1Y` |
| **Dividend token** | Collects the dividends until maturity | Yield token, `YT`, e.g. `xMSFTc-YT-1Y` |
| **Unit** | One share token plus one dividend token, worth one share at deposit | `units = raw × I` |
| **Split / strip** | Turn tokenized shares into share and dividend tokens | `XeroVault.strip` |
| **Rejoin / recombine** | Turn a share token and a dividend token back into the stock | `XeroVault.recombine` |
| **Redeem** | Turn a share token into the stock after maturity | `XeroVault.redeem` |
| **Maturity** | The date share tokens become shares | `XeroVault.maturity` |
| **Discount** | How much less the share token costs than the share | `ytPrice / (ptPrice + ytPrice)` |
| **Expected dividend yield** | The yearly dividend rate the market prices in | `impliedYield = impliedRate × dividendIndex` |
| **Dividend index** | The multiplier with stock splits divided out | `I = M / S` |
| **Structural factor** | The combined effect of reported splits | `S` |
| **Frozen** | Paused because the multiplier changed in a way not yet explained | `VaultFrozen` |
| **Halted** | New deposits stopped after a corporate action Xero can't represent | `VaultHaltedForAction` |
