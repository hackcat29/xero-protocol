# Welcome to Xero Protocol

> **"Keep the stock. Trade the dividends."**

**Xero Protocol** is the decentralized yield-stripping protocol for tokenized real-world equities (RWA), built natively on **Base**. Dubbed **"The Pendle for Tokenized Stocks"**, Xero enables investors to separate any yield-bearing tokenized equity into two independent, tradable components:

1. **Principal Token (PT):** Entitles the holder to the underlying stock's capital value at maturity, stripped of all dividend growth.
2. **Yield Token (YT):** Entitles the holder to all dividend growth and multiplier escalations generated by the stock until maturity.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Why Base & Coinbase B20?**  
Unlike traditional ERC-20 yield-bearing tokens where yields are streamed as recurring cash transfers or balance rebases, Coinbase's B20 token standard wraps dividend compounding directly into an escalating multiplier (`uiMultiplier`). Xero deconstructs this multiplier on-chain, unlocking institutional fixed-income and dividend speculation primitives for equities on Base.
{% endhint %}

## 🏛️ The Three Core Financial Primitives

Xero turns tokenized stocks into modular financial primitives:

```
                      ┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
                      │  Coinbase B20 Stock Token (e.g. NVDAc)  │
                      └────────────────────┬────────────────────┘
                                           │ Deposit in Vault
                                           ▼
                      ┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
                      │              Xero Vault                 │
                      └─────────────┬─────────────────────┬─────┘
                                    │                     │
                     Mints 1:1      ▼                     ▼  Mints 1:1
              ┌───────────────────────────┐         ┌───────────────────────────┐
              │   Principal Token (PT)    │         │     Yield Token (YT)      │
              │  "Capital at a Discount"  │         │   "Leveraged Dividends"   │
              └─────────────┬─────────────┘         └─────────────┬─────────────┘
                            │                                     │
                            ▼                                     ▼
                  Buy at a fixed discount               Sell upfront for fixed APY
               Redeem 1:1 for share at maturity      Trade for 50x–120x dividend leverage
```

### 1. Fixed Yield Sourcing
Long-term stock holders and treasuries deposit their B20 equity, mint PT + YT, and sell the YT upfront on Xero's AMM. They lock in **guaranteed fixed annual APY today** with **zero dividend volatility**.

### 2. Discounted Stock Buying
Investors buy Principal Tokens (PT) at an upfront discount to spot price (e.g., buying `NVDAc` at 8.4% off spot). At maturity, 1 PT redeems **1:1 for the full underlying share**.

### 3. Leveraged Dividend Speculation
Traders buy Yield Tokens (YT) for a fraction of the share price (e.g., 1.5% of spot), gaining **50x to 120x cash flow leverage** on corporate dividend hikes, special payouts, and distribution catalysts.

## ⚡ Key Highlights

* **Stock Split Immunity:** An attested structural factor ($S$) isolates forward stock splits from dividends, guaranteeing that stock splits do not drain vaults via phantom yield.
* **Continuous Multiplier Delta Accrual:** Yield is streamed continuously via $Y = \text{Tokens Held} \times \left(\frac{M_{current}}{M_{last\_claim}} - 1\right)$.
* **Yield-Space AMM:** Custom time-decay AMM ($P_{YT} = r \times \tau$) where YT prices smoothly converge to zero at maturity without arbitrage divergence loss.
* **Native Base Execution:** Sub-second transaction settlement and sub-cent gas fees on Base (OP Stack L2).
