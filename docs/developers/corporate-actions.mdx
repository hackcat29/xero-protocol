---
title: "Corporate actions"
description: "How splits and other corporate actions are reported and applied."
---

Tokenized stocks express dividends **and** splits through the same multiplier. Xero must credit only dividends to dividend tokens, so structural changes are reported to the `CorporateActionEngine` and divided out by every vault.

## Reporting

```solidity
function report(
    address token,
    ActionType kind,
    uint64 effectiveAt,
    uint128 value,
    uint128 value2,
    bytes32 ref
) external; // REPORTER_ROLE
```

An action becomes effective once `quorum` distinct reporters submit the identical payload. `ref` is a unique identifier from the data source, so the same action can't be applied twice.

| Constraint | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Cash dividends may be backdated by at most | 1 day |
| Structural actions may be backdated by at most | 30 days, so a missed split can always be resolved |
| Actions may be reported ahead by at most | 120 days |
| Largest split ratio | 100 : 1 |
| Largest multiplier growth one cash dividend may authorise | 50% |

## Action types

| Type | Handling |
| --- | --- |
| `CASH_DIVIDEND` | Authorises the vault's index to rise by the reinvested amount |
| `STOCK_DIVIDEND`, `FORWARD_SPLIT`, `REVERSE_SPLIT` | Structural: divided out of the multiplier, so value is unchanged |
| `NAME_CHANGE` | No effect on balances |
| `UNIT_SPLIT`, `SPIN_OFF`, `CASH_MERGER`, `STOCK_MERGER`, `STOCK_AND_CASH_MERGER`, `REDEMPTION`, `WORTHLESS_REMOVAL`, `RIGHTS_DISTRIBUTION` | Unsupported: the guardian halts new deposits for the stock. Existing positions can always exit |

## What vaults do

On each `sync`, a vault applies newly effective actions in order, then checks the multiplier:

- Explained growth (a reported dividend, or within `maxGrowthPerSync`) is credited to dividend tokens.
- A reported split updates the structural factor `S`, leaving the dividend index unchanged.
- Anything unexplained **freezes** the vault until it's reported. Recombination stays open.

## The keeper

`services/keeper` is a Node service that watches issuer corporate-action data, cross-checks it against each token's pending multiplier, and submits reports. Run several independent keepers with distinct keys to meet the quorum.

<Note>On Base Sepolia the quorum is 1 and the test issuer reports its own simulated actions.</Note>
